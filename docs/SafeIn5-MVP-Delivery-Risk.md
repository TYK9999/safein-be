# SafeIn5 MVP — Delivery, Risk & Sequencing

**Prepared from:** `SafeIn5 Dev Pack V9 Issue.md` · `SafeIn5 MVP Requirements Specification and Tender Pack v1.0.md` · `Talentica Proposal- SafeIn5.md` · `SafeIn5.md` · `SafeIn5-PRD.md`

**Position in one line:** the Talentica milestone plan is a *feature-completion* plan, and SafeIn5 has bought a *validation* MVP whose primary success measure is repeat usage. Those two things want different build orders. The plan below keeps the 14 weeks, the 5 milestones and the £45,000, and changes only the order of work and the acceptance gates — because the current order defers every irreversible decision (anonymity, tenancy, retention, telemetry) and every high-risk unknown (QR end-to-end, iOS PWA, industrial-noise STT, quarry connectivity) to the second half of the project.

---

## 1. Recommended build sequence, and the case for resequencing

### 1.1 What Talentica proposed

| Milestone | Weeks | Content as proposed |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Discovery & UX | 1–2 | Workshops, journeys, UX, scope prioritisation |
| M2 Foundation & PULSE | 3–6 | Tenant setup, users, auth, sites, PWA shell, PULSE, capture, media |
| M3 Context, Learning & Workflow | 7–9 | Learn5 mgmt, **QR management, QR-driven journeys**, feed, search, workflow foundation |
| M4 Supervisor, Analytics & Platform | 10–12 | Supervisor workflows, notifications, analytics, moderation, **GDPR compliance**, **anonymous reporting**, community feed, worker feedback loop |
| M5 UAT & Sign-off | 13–14 | UAT, defect resolution, **production rollout** |

### 1.2 Five things are in the wrong place, and four of them are irreversible

**(a) Anonymity is in M4. It is a write-time schema property.**
Dev Pack §7 says anonymity is delivered by "stripping user metadata at the DB level"; the proposal (NFR row) says non-reversibility is out of MVP. Whichever position wins, it decides whether `behaviour_signal` carries an author FK, a nulled FK, or a per-tenant one-way pseudonym. That column is written in migration 0001. Building anonymity in week 11 means either rewriting the signal table and reprocessing every row created in weeks 4–10, or shipping the reversible version by default because nobody wanted to migrate. **Anonymity moves to M2, week 4.**

**(b) GDPR is in M4. Erasure, retention and audit-log tombstoning are schema properties too.**
SCP-052 (Delete/Block User, MUST) and SCP-058 (Delete observations, SHOULD) exist as bare capability names with no semantics. Deciding in week 11 how erasure coexists with "append-only audit logging" (proposal NFR) means redesigning the tables built in week 5. **The erasure/retention model moves to M2; the *screens* can stay in M4.**

**(c) QR is in M3. It is the product's primary entry point and its largest unknown.**
Dev Pack §8.1: "QR is a core product feature and must be treated as a primary entry point, not a secondary feature." Dev Pack §13 makes QR→PULSE/Rescue Plan/Learn5 an acceptance condition. The client's own canonical journey (`SafeIn5.md` Step 1) *starts* with a scan. Yet in the proposed plan nobody scans a physical sticker on a real phone at a real site until week 7 at the earliest, and the ~30 mappings and the physical placement are SafeIn5's operational responsibility (QA-004) with no date attached. Every unknown in QR — camera permissions on locked-down rugged Android, context resolution latency on a quarry link, what a stale or unknown code shows, whether unauthenticated resolution leaks corporate content — is discovered in week 7+. **A working QR resolve→PULSE route moves into the week-3 skeleton.**

**(d) The closure loop is in M4. It is the mechanism that produces the primary success metric.**
Dev Pack §17.1 Scenario 5 ("Report Black Hole Risk") names unacknowledged reports as the failure that collapses engagement, and PRD §5.4 has already selected Acknowledge + Close. If acknowledgement only exists from week 12 and the pilot only runs after week 14, no worker ever experiences the feedback loop during the engagement window the project is supposed to measure. Repeat usage is a *behavioural* metric; it is generated by the loop, not by the capture screen. **Acknowledge + Close moves to M3, week 8.**

**(e) Production rollout is inside M5, alongside UAT, defect fixing and handover — in two weeks.**
Two weeks cannot contain UAT, defect resolution, first-ever production stand-up (domain, TLS, WAF, secrets, DB sizing, backup/restore test, runbook) and handover. And there is *no pilot window at all*: the engagement ends at MVP sign-off, so "repeat usage" — the stated primary measure (Dev Pack §14, Success Metrics MUST, QA-001) — cannot be observed before the money is paid. **Production stands up in M4; M5 becomes cutover + UAT; a live pilot window is agreed as a separate, pre-signed SOW.**

**(f) Telemetry is in M4 with "analytics". The 60-second criterion is measured by telemetry.**
Dev Pack §4 makes <60s capture and <10s classification mandatory rules whose failure "invalidates the product model", and §13 makes them acceptance items. The evidence of record must be instrumented client-side timing events. If those events are built in week 11, there is no baseline from weeks 3–10 and no chance to fix a slow flow. **The timing event schema ships with the walking skeleton in week 3.**

## 2. The walking skeleton

**Definition:** by end of week 3, a person can scan a printed QR code with an ordinary phone, land in PULSE, capture a photo with a caption, classify it, and see it in a feed — deployed on staging, on a real domain, over TLS, with timing telemetry recording every step. One tenant, one site, one sub-site, one QR mapping, one Learn5 card, one rescue plan.

### 2.1 What is real

| Component | Real in W3 |
|---|---|
| QR code | A **printed sticker** with a real short URL resolving server-side to a context record. Not a dev deep link. |
| Context resolution | Real DB lookup: site, sub-site, task type, risk type, destination set. Returns a tenant-scoped payload. |
| PULSE | Real sequential five-step flow with real copy for one context |
| Capture | Real camera, real photo, real async background upload to real object storage, real signed URL read |
| Classification | Real three-option one-tap classify, real draft→finalised transition |
| Feed | Real query, filtered to the scanned sub-site, real RLS predicate |
| Telemetry | Real `capture_start / draft_created / classification_selected / signal_finalised / qr_resolved / context_journey_started` events with device, connection type, tenant |
| Deployment | Real CI → staging via IaC. Every subsequent week deploys the same way. |

### 2.2 What it deliberately fakes

Faking these is the point. Each is a decision that is either still open with the client or is not on the critical technical-risk path.

| Faked | Fake used in W3 | When it becomes real |
|---|---|---|
| **Authentication** | A single seeded user, no login screen | W5 |
| **Multi-tenancy** | One hardcoded org; RLS policy present but only one tenant to isolate | W4 |
| **Anonymity** | Toggle rendered but non-functional and labelled "not yet wired" | W4 |
| **Admin console** | QR mapping, site, Learn5 and rescue plan seeded by SQL fixtures | W7–W8 |
| **Learn5 / Rescue Plan content** | One placeholder card and one placeholder plan written by Talentica, visibly marked SAMPLE | W8, when SafeIn5 supplies real content |
| **Voice-to-text** | Voice records and attaches; transcript is a stub string | W6 |
| **Supervisor workflow** | Absent | W8 |
| **Notifications** | Absent | Deferred (§4) |
| **Community tenant** | Absent | W10 |
| **Search** | Absent (feed filter only) | First scope cut candidate (§5) |
| **Media processing** | Full-size image, no thumbnail, no EXIF strip | W6 (EXIF strip is a privacy requirement, not polish) |

### 2.3 Why this specific slice

It is the exact chain in Dev Pack §13 Definition of Acceptance ("Enter the app / Capture in under 60 seconds / Classify it / See it appear in the feed") plus the §13.2 QR condition, minus everything that is a *decision* rather than a *risk*. It answers, in week 3 instead of week 9, the four questions that can actually kill the project: does a scan open the app fast enough on the devices workers carry; does camera capture work through gloves; does an async upload survive a quarry cell link; and is 60 seconds achievable at all. If the answer to any of those is no, there are eleven weeks left to react rather than one.

## 4. Scope-cut ladder

Ordered. Cut in this sequence if the timeline slips. Each cut is justified against the Success Metrics sheet, because the only defensible cutting rule on a validation MVP is *"does the metric survive?"*

**Never cut, at any point:** sub-60s capture · three-option classification · feed · QR resolve → PULSE / Rescue Plan / Learn5 · anonymity model · timing telemetry · RLS tenant isolation · Acknowledge. These are Dev Pack §13 acceptance conditions or the load-bearing structure of the MUST metrics.

| # | Cut | What survives | Metric impact | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Postgres full-text search** | Feed filter by classification and sub-site (WRK-014) | **None.** No Success Metrics row depends on search. | At ~60 users over a 6-week pilot the corpus is a few hundred signals in a chronological feed. Search is a scale feature solving a problem the pilot does not have. Dev Pack §9 lists it, so this is a formal (small) variation. |
| **2** | **Video capture** | Photo + voice note + text | Signal Submissions unaffected — the *type* split (Good Practice / Be Aware / Needs Attention) is by classification, not media. | CQA-007 is self-contradictory: priority MUST, phase **Phase 2**. Photo is "core" and voice is "preferred" per CQA-006; video is neither. Removing it removes a 30s payload on a patchy quarry uplink, transcode work, and storage growth — i.e. it *helps* the 60-second metric. Highest ratio of saved effort to lost validation in the whole build. |
| **3** | **Web push notifications (WRK-022)** | In-app notification centre + feed badge on next open | **None.** No metric measures notification delivery. | WRK-022's decision columns are verbatim copies of WRK-020 (voice-to-text) — nobody ever adjudicated it. It depends on ADM-041/042 cluster detection, both **WON'T**; WRK-023 says "predictive alerts should not be in MVP"; the proposal itself excludes auto-generated push. ## 6. Measurement plan

All eight Success Metrics rows have empty "Tenderer Response / Proposed Implementation" cells. Here is the proposed fill.

**Architecture.** All metrics are computed **server-side** from the transactional outbox already in the proposed architecture (Slide 19). No third-party analytics on the guest QR path. Every metric below is a SQL query over first-party event tables — which is precisely why Clarity is unnecessary (R5). Reported as a weekly aggregate CSV + a static metrics page.

**The identity primitive that makes anonymous measurement possible.** Every Behaviour Signal carries `reporter_pseudonym = HMAC(tenant_salt, user_id)` — a per-tenant one-way token — alongside `author_id`, which is **null** on anonymous signals. The pseudonym supports equality matching (repeat usage, abuse throttling) but not identification, and it does not cross tenant boundaries. This single column is why anonymity must be settled in W4: it is the difference between an MVP that can report its own primary success metric and one that cannot.

| # | Metric | Priority | Instrumentation | Reported as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Active Users** | MUST | `session_started` event keyed on `user_id`, deduplicated per day. Guests excluded (they have no account and cannot submit per PRD §5.6) and counted separately as *guest sessions*. | DAU / WAU per site and per org; % of the ~50–60 invited cohort active in the last 7 days |
| 2 | **Repeat Usage** | MUST | **Definition:** a distinct `reporter_pseudonym` that has finalised **≥2 Behaviour Signals on ≥2 distinct calendar days**. ## 7. Definition of Done, and the defect model

### 7.1 Universal DoD (every milestone)

Applies to all five. A milestone is not submitted for sign-off until all of these hold.

1. Automated test suite green in CI — unit, API integration (Testcontainers), and Playwright E2E for the milestone's journeys.
2. **Cross-tenant isolation test suite green** — every RLS-protected read attempted from a foreign tenant returns empty or 403. From M2 onward, non-negotiable (R17).
3. SAST + dependency scan clean of high/critical.
4. Deployed to staging **from CI via IaC** — no hand-built environments, ever.
5. Migrations forward-and-rollback tested against a copy of staging data.
6. Zero mandatory fields verified on every capture path added in the milestone.
7. Structured logging and telemetry events emitted for every new user-facing action.
8. Milestone demo performed **on a real pilot device**, not a desktop browser emulator.
9. Open P1/P2 defect count = 0.

### 7.3 Defect severity and SLA model

The rule that matters: **a defect is behaviour not matching the agreed requirement or design; a change is a new or altered requirement.** Write that sentence into the contract. Without it, M5 — which holds 15% of the fee and is described only as "defect resolution" — is a two-week argument.

| Sev | Definition | Response | Fix target | Gates sign-off? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Critical** | Worker cannot capture, classify or submit a signal · data loss · **anonymity breach** · **cross-tenant data exposure** · QR at a confined space fails to reach the Rescue Plan · production down | 4 working hours | 1 working day | **Yes** |
| **P2 — Major** | A MUST requirement materially not working with no workaround · **the 60s or 10s NFR missed on the reference device** · media upload fails on a supported browser · supervisor cannot acknowledge · admin cannot create a site/user/QR mapping | 1 working day | 3 working days | **Yes** |
| **P3 — Minor** | SHOULD requirement degraded, or a MUST with an acceptable workaround · cosmetic issues on secondary screens · non-blocking admin inconvenience | 3 working days | Batched; fixed within M5 where capacity allows, otherwise logged to the post-MVP backlog | **No** |
| **P4 — Trivial** | Copy, spacing, iconography, nice-to-haves, enhancement ideas | Logged | Post-MVP T&M backlog | **No** |

**Provisions to agree with the model:**

- **The mandatory design rules are P1/P2 acceptance items, not aspirations.** Dev Pack §4 states failure to meet them "invalidates the product model" — so they must have an enforcement mechanism. p90 capture >60s on the agreed reference device and network profile is a **P2**; zero-mandatory-fields violated anywhere is a **P2**; an anonymity breach is a **P1**.
- **Only P1 and P2 gate milestone or final sign-off.** P3/P4 are logged and do not withhold payment.
- **Platform constraints are not defects.** iOS background-upload suspension, absence of Background Sync, web push requiring home-screen install, IndexedDB eviction — accepted in writing at M1 as Apple platform behaviour (R6) and not raisable as UAT defects.
- **Anything raised in UAT that is not in the signed baseline is a change request**, priced under Assumption 4 and routed to the post-MVP T&M team — not absorbed into the same two-week window that carries the final 15%.
- **Client-side SLAs are symmetrical:** milestone sign-off 3–5 business days, final sign-off 1–2 weeks, change acknowledgement 1–2 days, impact analysis 2–3 days, approval <3 days (Slide 24). Breach of a client SLA or a dependency due date (§6) gives day-for-day schedule relief.

## Summary of the recommendation

Keep the price, keep the 14 weeks, keep the five milestones and the payment schedule. Change four things:

1. **Settle the governing scope baseline before signature.** Two documents each claim to be the pricing baseline, and one of them (Dev Pack V9) contradicts itself between §5, §7 and §9. This is not a discovery item; it is a contract term.
2. **Move every irreversible decision into M2 and every unknown into week 3.** Anonymity, tenancy, retention and telemetry are schema; QR, iOS, STT-in-noise and quarry connectivity are risks. Neither belongs in the second half of a 14-week project.
3. **Move production into M4 and add a live pilot window after M5.** Repeat usage is the primary success measure and it cannot be observed at the moment of sign-off. Delivering the MVP in full and still not answering the question it was commissioned to answer is the most likely failure mode of this engagement.
4. **Pre-agree the scope-cut ladder and the defect model at M1.** Cuts 1–3 (search, video, push) are effectively free and should be taken proactively. Everything below cut 4 should be a conversation held in week 2, not week 12.
