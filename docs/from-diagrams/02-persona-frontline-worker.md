# Persona 1: Frontline Worker

Source: persona design notes.

## Role in system

- Primary user of the PULSE loop; source of Behavioural Signals

## Core actions

- Completes Take 5 (pre-task behavioural pause) before starting work
- Follows PULSE: Pause → Uncover → Learn → Steer → Echo
- Captures Behaviour Signals via photo, video, or text
- Scans QR codes at entry points (e.g. confined space) → routed to PULSE prompt, Rescue Plan, Learn 5 content
- Classifies own signal in &lt;10 seconds: Good Practice / Something to be aware of / Needs attention now
- Consumes Learn 5 micro-learning content
- Can opt into anonymous / confidential reporting

## Goals / motivations

- Make safer decisions “at the point of work in under five minutes”
- Share observations that “could help others stay safe”
- Complete tasks with minimal friction — “capture first, structure later”

## Constraints on their experience (mandatory design rules)

- Zero mandatory fields at capture
- Capture completable in under 60 seconds
- No long forms or structured input
- UX must feel like WhatsApp (low friction)
- Must work offline, sync later

## Pain points / fears (from scenarios)

- Fears retaliation if management sees their identity (Scenario: Unsafe Scaffold Access)
- Doesn't want to identify colleagues directly (Scenario: PPE Non-Compliance)
- Risk of disengagement if signals go unacknowledged — **Report Black Hole Risk**: “No visible action or feedback is returned… Engagement and trust begin to collapse”

## What they receive back

- Acknowledgement or closure feedback once a signal is actioned
- Positive reinforcement / confirmation on sharing (“Thanks for sharing!”)
- Reinforcement messaging after classification (Frame 07)

## System's stance toward them (ethical principle)

- Platform is “not a surveillance or disciplinary platform”
- Focus is on conditions / behaviours, not blaming individuals
- Designed for “psychological safety and optional anonymity”
