# Behavior Incentive

Source: behavior-incentive design notes.

## What the product actually builds in (real incentive mechanics)

### 1. Visible closure — the single most emphasized lever

- Failure mode: **Report Black Hole Risk** — users submit repeatedly, get no feedback, and engagement / trust collapse
- Fix: Status Update (user notified of progress), Community Comments, and “Better Community” outcome messaging
- Incentive is not a reward — it is avoiding the silence that kills return usage
- Treated as **mandatory**, not optional

### 2. Immediate positive reinforcement at the moment of action

- Frame 07 (Confirmation): “Thanks for sharing! Your signal helps keep everyone safe”
- Plus a “View Feed” prompt
- Rewards the act of contributing, independent of whether anything is fixed yet

### 3. Removal of friction and fear (lowers the cost of returning)

- Sub-60-second capture, zero mandatory fields
- Anonymous / confidential mode removes social risk
- “Trust, not blame” / condition-first (not person-first) reporting removes fear of retaliation
- People do not return to systems that felt punishing or effortful last time

### 4. Social proof / seeing your action matter in a shared space

- Feed display (“What's being shared”), public map view
- Scenario 5 (Positive Community Safety Sharing): constructive examples become behavioural reinforcement in the ecosystem
- Social / civic identity: “I'm someone who contributes to a safer place”

### 5. Learning as a secondary reward loop

- Learn 5 content tied contextually to what you just reported
- Gives a reason to open the app beyond reporting

## Other ideas (not necessarily in the document)

### Recognition & status (low-risk, fits ethos)

- **Contributor tiers** — milestone badges tied to consistency, not volume (e.g. “reported 5 weeks running” vs “most reports”)
- **Signal of the week** — team / site highlights a Good Practice signal, credited with opt-in (not anonymous)
- **Site / team-level leaderboards** — collective safety score per site, not per-worker ranking

### Closing the loop, amplified

- **“You made a difference” tracing** — “Your signal led to X being fixed”
- **Aggregate impact stats** — “signals from your site prevented 3 escalations this month”

### Habit-formation mechanics

- **Streaks tied to Take 5, not reporting** — reward consistent pre-task pauses; do not incentivize manufacturing signals
- **Contextual nudges** — QR scan patterns trigger light “welcome back” / streak notes

### Learning-linked rewards

- **Micro-certifications** — completing a cluster of Learn 5 on a topic unlocks a badge / competency marker
- **Personalized Learn 5 surfacing** — content adapts to what a user has reported
