# Community / Civic Identity

Source: community-identity and anti-pattern notes.

## Community / civic identity

- **“Community Guardian” framing** — position frequent-but-genuine contributors as informal safety stewards, publicly (with consent) — leans into identity (“I'm the kind of person who looks out for others”) rather than transactional reward
- **Cross-community visibility (public / community layer)** — showing that a reported hazard was seen and acted on by a local authority builds civic trust, similar to citizen-reporting apps (potholes, etc.)

## What to explicitly avoid

- **Cash / point-redeemable rewards for volume** — risks “snitch culture” and gamed-reporting problems
- **Public shaming or negative leaderboards** — contradicts “non-punitive” and “condition-first not person-first” principles
- **Anything that could turn Needs Attention Now reports into a competition** — urgency-based signals should never feel like they are being farmed for points
