**SafeIn5 – Rigging & Fixtures User Scenario (Phase 1)**

**User Persona**

**Name:** Dave\
**Role:** Senior Rigger / Fixture Installation Technician\
**Environment:** Heavy fabrication yard / shutdown maintenance site / offshore prep area\
**Device:** Personal mobile phone or company-issued rugged device\
**Connectivity:** Intermittent WiFi / patchy cellular network

**Scenario: Unsafe Lifting Fixture Setup Before a Critical Lift**

**Context**

Dave and his crew are preparing a heavy fixture lift using a custom spreader beam and chain assembly.

The team is under schedule pressure because the crane window is limited.

At the lifting area entrance, there is a QR code attached to the lifting zone barrier.

The QR is linked to:

- Lift-specific PULSE (Take 5)

- Rescue / emergency procedure

- Learn 5 content for suspended loads and pinch points

**Step-by-Step User Journey**

**Step 1 — QR Scan Before Starting Work**

Dave scans the QR code using his phone.

The system immediately opens the SafeIn5 PULSE screen.

The app already knows:

- Site

- Lift zone

- Asset ID

- Task type = Heavy Lift

- Risk type = Suspended Load

Dave does not need to type anything.

**User Experience Goal**

Fast entry into context with zero friction.

**Step 2 — PULSE (Take 5)**

Dave sees a short behavioural pause flow:

**P – Pause**

“Stop before the lift starts.”

**U – Uncover**

“What looks different today?”

Dave notices:

- One shackle orientation looks unusual

- Tag line placement may create a pinch point

**L – Learn**

A short Learn 5 card appears:\
“Incorrect shackle loading can create side-loading failure.”

**S – Steer**

Dave asks the team to reposition the tag line.

**E – Echo**

The app prompts:

“Share this moment so others stay safe?”

**Step 3 — Capture a Behaviour Signal**

Dave taps:\
“Share Observation”

The camera opens instantly.

He takes:

- 1 photo of the rigging arrangement

- Optional short voice/text note:\
  “Potential side loading on shackle before lift.”

No forms.

No dropdown overload.

Capture takes under 30 seconds.

Even though the upload is still processing in the background, Dave can continue immediately.

**Step 4 — Classification**

The app asks one simple question:

“What best describes this?”

Dave selects:

“Something to be aware of”

That is the only required action.

The signal is now finalized.

**Step 5 — Confirmation & Reinforcement**

SafeIn5 shows:

“Thanks. You helped improve lift safety for the next crew.”

The interaction feels supportive rather than compliance-driven.

No blame.\
No naming individuals.\
No disciplinary tone.

**Step 6 — Feed Visibility**

Later during break, another rigging crew on the same site sees the signal in the feed.

They can see:

- Photo

- Context

- Classification

- Lift zone

- Time shared

But not personal identity (if anonymous mode is enabled).

The second crew realizes they are using a similar shackle orientation on another fixture.

The learning spreads organically.

**Step 7 — Supervisor Awareness (Phase 1 Light Workflow)**

The lifting supervisor reviews the feed.

He notices:

- 3 similar “Be Aware” signals in two weeks

- All related to custom lifting fixtures

Even without AI analytics in Phase 1, the trend becomes visible manually.

Supervisor actions:

- Toolbox talk next morning

- Temporary lift check added

- Shared Learn 5 link redistributed

**What Makes This Valuable for Rig & Fixture Teams**

**1. Fits Existing Work Rhythm**

Workers already:

- Scan permits

- Check lift plans

- Review JSAs

- Do Take 5 pauses

SafeIn5 fits naturally into this process.

**2. Removes Reporting Friction**

Traditional systems fail because they require:

- Long forms

- Logins

- Incident categorization

- Fear of blame

SafeIn5 allows:

- Photo first

- Minimal interaction

- Context auto-attached

- Under 60 seconds

**3. Captures Near-Miss Intelligence Early**

Rigging environments constantly generate:

- Temporary unsafe conditions

- Workarounds

- Setup deviations

- Environmental changes

Most never become formal incidents.

SafeIn5 captures these micro-signals before something serious happens.

**4. Creates “Shared Awareness”**

The biggest value is not reporting upward.

It is:\
Crew-to-crew learning.

Example:

- One crew identifies pinch risk

- Another crew avoids repeating it

- Site awareness improves continuously

**Phase 1 Features Used in This Scenario**

Included:

- QR contextual routing

- PULSE flow

- Behaviour Signal capture

- Media upload

- Classification

- Feed

- Learn 5

- Basic context mapping

Not Used Yet:

- AI pattern detection

- Advanced dashboards

- Enterprise integrations

- Predictive analytics
